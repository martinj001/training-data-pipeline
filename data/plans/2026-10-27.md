with no pain means it's a real working weight now, not a re-test. Legs intentionally

## Running Support Routine

**Pre-run warm-up (5 min, dynamic) — before every run, including travel runs**
- 30 sec high knees
- 30 sec butt kicks
- 10 walking lunges each leg
- 10 leg swings each direction, each leg (front/back, then side/side)
- 10 bodyweight glute bridges
- 20 sec ankle circles each foot

**Post-run stretch (8–10 min, static, hold 30 sec each side) — after every run**
- Calf stretch (wall)
- Hip flexor stretch (kneeling lunge)
- Quad stretch (standing)
- Hamstring stretch (standing or seated)
- IT band / glute stretch (figure-4 or pigeon)

## Strength Sessions

### Session A — Upper (~55 min; ~30 min travel version Week 2)

**Warm-up (5 min) — keep it pain-free, not a test**
- Arm circles, band pull-aparts (light), 10 scapular push-ups, hip flexor stretch.

**KB row (single arm)**
- 3 × 10 @ 35 lbs — updated to match actual recent weight.

**DB Overhead Press — working set now, not a re-test**
- 3 × 8 @ 50 lbs/hand. Two clean sessions at 40–50 lbs means this is the real working
  weight. Any pinch/sharp pain in the left shoulder → stop immediately, drop OH press
  for the rest of the block.
- **Travel week (Nov 3): skip entirely — no dumbbells on the road.**

**Pull-ups**
- 3 × 8 @ bodyweight — holding; RPE hit 9.3 last time, close to a ceiling at this
  volume, not pushing further yet.
- **Travel week (Nov 3): skip — no bar.**

**Push-ups — full superset**
- 3 × 15 standard + 3 × 12 incline — holding. **Available while traveling.**

**Standing Calf Raise — bodyweight only, no bar**
- 3 × 15 @ bodyweight, 2-sec up, pause at top, 2-sec down. **Available while
  traveling.**

**Core finisher**
- 3 rounds: 9 hanging knee raises, 18 sec hollow hold
- 1 round push-up to downward dog
- 2 rounds: 70 sec plank (posterior tilt — tuck pelvis)
- **Travel week (Nov 3): sub lying leg raises for hanging knee raises (no bar), rest
  unchanged.**

## Trainer tempo — rotating options

Default leans toward the structured options (Sweet Spot / Over-Unders) rather than
easy Z3 — pick whichever fits the day, but favor 1 or 2 unless recovery says otherwise.
If riding outdoors instead (or FTS MTB is on), no structure required.

**Option 1 — Sweet Spot**
- 10 min easy warm-up (Z2)
- 2 × 15 min sweet spot (top of Z3 / bottom of Z4), 5 min Z2 recovery between
- 10 min cooldown

**Option 2 — Over-Unders**
- 10 min easy warm-up (Z2)
- 3 × 10 min alternating 2 min over-threshold (Z4) / 2 min under (Z3), 5 min Z2
  recovery between sets
- 10 min cooldown

**Option 3 — Classic Z3 tempo (fallback / lower-key day)**
- 10 min easy warm-up (Z2)
- 4 × 8 min Z3 ("comfortably hard" — can talk, wouldn't want to), 5 min Z2 recovery
  between
- 5 min cooldown
