# Training Plan: September 21 – October 4, 2026

**Focus:** Resuming a formal block after an unplanned gap week (Sep 14–20, no written
plan — training continued informally: 3 runs, 1 Zwift ride, 1 leg session). New
constraint this block: a **SIRVA (Shoulder Injury Related to Vaccine Administration)**
in the left shoulder from a recent vaccination — the injection hit a tendon and it's
not back to full force. Session A is rebuilt around that: no overhead pressing at all,
and pull-ups/push-ups are being tested cautiously rather than assumed safe. Legs hold
flat at the numbers actually proven (not the old written block-8 ladder).

## Notes

- **SIRVA (left shoulder) rule:** any sharp pain, or any movement that noticeably
  loads/activates the left deltoid → stop that exercise immediately, move to the next
  one or end the session early. **No overhead pressing this block, full stop** — not
  reduced weight, removed entirely. Pull-ups are untested since the injury; Tuesday
  09-22 is the first real data point. Push-ups are expected to be uncomfortable —
  starting incline-only (lower shoulder demand than flat) rather than assuming the
  usual push-up superset is fine.
- **Standing Calf Raise drops the barbell this block** — previously done with a bar
  racked across the upper back; switched to bodyweight-only so nothing loads the
  shoulders incidentally on a movement that isn't even about the shoulders. Not
  time-limited to this block specifically — no real reason to bring the bar back later
  either, it wasn't a progression lift anyway (see the standing 07-21 call on calf
  raise being maintenance, not a lift to load).
- **Squat/RDL hold at the two most recently proven numbers, not block 8's old ladder.**
  The two ad hoc sessions during the gap week (09-13, 09-19) both ran Squat 3×10 @
  115/135/155 and RDL 3×10 @ 65/95/105 cleanly, no pain flagged — using that as this
  block's baseline rather than reverting to block 8's written 4-set/160-top version,
  which was never actually run at that volume this cycle. No RPE was logged on either
  ad hoc session, so this is a hold, not a progression call — start logging RPE again
  this block so the next bump is a real decision, not a guess.
- **Runs continue the +5 min/week ramp** — 40 min this week, 45 min next, continuing
  toward the standing 60-min goal. Last three real data points (09-15, 09-16, 09-18)
  were 38, 36, 35 min, all clean.
- **Recovery was choppy early in the gap week, strong by the end.** Sep 14–17: 4
  straight red days (42–46%). Sep 18–20: 70%, 61%, 83% — a good place to open the block
  from. Standing policy: keep sessions on the calendar regardless of score, per
  usual — but the SIRVA caution above is about the shoulder specifically, not recovery.
- **Group rides and MTB always trump the plan.**
- Sat and Sun can swap — both are cardio slots.
- Monday 09-21 is off — errands.

## Strength Sessions

### Session A — Upper, SIRVA-modified (~45 min Week 1, ~55 min if progressing Week 2)

**Warm-up (5 min) — keep it pain-free, not a test**
- Arm circles, band pull-aparts (light), 10 scapular push-ups, hip flexor stretch.
  Stop any warm-up movement that provokes the left shoulder — this is a warm-up, the
  actual test happens in the exercises below.

**KB row (single arm) — first movement, lowest shoulder risk**
- 3 × 10 @ 40 lbs — unchanged from block 8, but still watch the left side on the row.

**Pull-ups — cautious first test**
- Week 1: 2 × 6 (down from the usual 4×8) — first real data point since the injury.
  Any left shoulder pain → stop here, skip pull-ups for the rest of the block.
- Week 2: 3 × 8 **only if Week 1 was clean.** If Week 1 flagged anything, hold at 2×6
  or drop pull-ups entirely this block — don't auto-progress through pain.

**Push-ups — incline only, no flat push-ups yet**
- Week 1: 2 rounds × 10 incline push-ups (lower shoulder demand than flat). Flat
  push-ups are paused, not cancelled — revisit once incline is clean.
- Week 2: if Week 1 was clean, back to the full superset (3 × 15 standard + 3 × 12
  incline). If not, hold at incline-only.

**Standing Calf Raise — bodyweight only, no bar**
- 3 × 15 @ bodyweight, 2-sec up, pause at top, 2-sec down. No barbell across the
  shoulders this block (or going forward — see notes above).

**Barbell Overhead Press — removed this block.** Not reduced weight — removed
entirely. Revisit reintroducing it in a future block review once the shoulder's
actually settled, not on a fixed timeline.

**Core finisher (both weeks)**
- 3 rounds: 9 hanging knee raises, 18 sec hollow hold
- 1 round push-up to downward dog — also shoulder-adjacent, same stop-if-pain rule
- 2 rounds: 70 sec plank (posterior tilt — tuck pelvis)

## Trainer tempo — rotating options

Default leans toward the structured options (Sweet Spot / Over-Unders) rather than
easy Z3 — pick whichever fits the day, but favor 1 or 2 unless recovery says otherwise.
If riding outdoors instead (or FTS MTB is on), no structure required.

**Option 1 — Sweet Spot**
- 10 min easy warm-up (Z2)
- 2 × 15 min sweet spot (top of Z3 / bottom of Z4), 5 min Z2 recovery between
- 10 min cooldown

**Option 2 — Over-Unders**
- 10 min easy warm-up (Z2)
- 3 × 10 min alternating 2 min over-threshold (Z4) / 2 min under (Z3), 5 min Z2
  recovery between sets
- 10 min cooldown

**Option 3 — Classic Z3 tempo (fallback / lower-key day)**
- 10 min easy warm-up (Z2)
- 4 × 8 min Z3 ("comfortably hard" — can talk, wouldn't want to), 5 min Z2 recovery
  between
- 5 min cooldown
