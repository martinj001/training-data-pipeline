# Training Plan: August 31 – September 13, 2026

**Focus:** First full block back after the 08-12 appendectomy. Cleared by the surgeon with some caution, self-managing day to day. Treating this as a reintroduction block — main lifts resume at the Aug 3–16 block's prescribed working weights (holding flat, no new progression), core work eases back in gradually across the two weeks instead of jumping straight to full volume, and running picks back up the ramp toward 45 min that was interrupted by the 08-07 hamstring pull and then surgery.

## Notes
- **Squat's 160 top set and RDL's 4-set bump were never actually tested.** Both were prescribed in the Aug 3–16 plan, but that block's only Session B (Fri 08-14) was cancelled for surgery before either could be run. This block is genuinely the first live data point on those numbers, not a confirmed-clean repeat — watch RPE and any hamstring/glute signal closely on Session B, especially Week 1.
- **Core work eases back in over two weeks, not full volume immediately.** Week 1: planks and hanging knee raises at roughly half the prior volume/duration. Week 2: stepped back up toward (but not quite at) full. Squat, RDL, OHP, KB work all resume at full pre-surgery weight since those don't load the incision the way core work does — see the reduced numbers in each session below.
- **OHP still unconfirmed at 50 lbs.** The Aug 11 session that was supposed to confirm RPE had settled was never actually logged (blank workbook — likely just missed, not a bad sign either way). Holding at 50 again rather than assuming it's settled.
- **Recovery has been choppy since surgery.** Aug 17–29: 45% avg recovery, HRV 52.2 avg [35.4–67.6], 5 of 13 days red (08-19, 08-21, 08-23, 08-27, 08-28) — rougher than any prior full block (previous roughest was 60% avg / 6 red days). Today (08-29): 70% recovery — good starting point heading in. Treating the choppiness as a data point to watch, not a reason to alter the calendar, per standing policy — but be more willing than usual to swap a day if something feels off.
- **Runs ramp +5 min/week** — 40 min this week, 45 min next week. Last real data point was the 37-min run on 08-23 (first since the injury/surgery layoff), which came back clean.
- **Group rides and MTB always trump the plan.**
- **Hamstring/glute pain rule stands:** sharp or pulling pain (not general effort burn) → stop immediately, swap in dead bug / core work instead.
- Sat and Sun can swap — both are cardio slots.

## Strength Sessions

### Session A — Upper / Push-Pull (~60 min)

**Warm-up (5 min)**
- Arm circles, band pull-aparts or shoulder CARs, 10 scapular push-ups, hip flexor stretch

**Pull-ups / Calf raises superset**
- 4 rounds: pull-ups × 8 → immediately calf raises × 15, rest 90 sec
- **Standing Barbell Calf Raise** (bar racked across upper back like a back squat), 2-sec up, pause at top, 2-sec down — bodyweight only (0 lbs), calf health/stretch maintenance, not a progression lift.

**Push-up superset**
- 3 rounds: 15 standard push-ups + 12 incline push-ups, rest 60 sec

**KB row (single arm)**
- 3 × 10 @ 40 lbs — hold, no progression this block.

**Barbell overhead press (standing)**
- 3 × 8 @ 50 lbs — still holding. Last block's confirmation session was never logged, so treat this as still-unsettled until proven otherwise.

**Core finisher — Week 1 (eased back, ~half volume)**
- 2 rounds: 8 hanging knee raises, 15 sec hollow hold
- 1 round push-up to downward dog (unchanged — not ab-loading)
- 2 rounds: 45 sec plank (posterior tilt — tuck pelvis)

**Core finisher — Week 2 (stepping back up)**
- 3 rounds: 9 hanging knee raises, 18 sec hollow hold
- 1 round push-up to downward dog
- 2 rounds: 70 sec plank

## Trainer tempo — rotating options

Default leans toward the structured options (Sweet Spot / Over-Unders) rather than easy Z3 — pick whichever fits the day, but favor 1 or 2 unless recovery says otherwise. If riding outdoors instead (or FTS MTB is on), no structure required.

**Option 1 — Sweet Spot**
- 10 min easy warm-up (Z2)
- 2 × 15 min sweet spot (top of Z3 / bottom of Z4), 5 min Z2 recovery between
- 10 min cooldown

**Option 2 — Over-Unders**
- 10 min easy warm-up (Z2)
- 3 × 10 min alternating 2 min over-threshold (Z4) / 2 min under (Z3), 5 min Z2 recovery between sets
- 10 min cooldown

**Option 3 — Classic Z3 tempo (fallback / lower-key day)**
- 10 min easy warm-up (Z2)
- 4 × 8 min Z3 ("comfortably hard" — can talk, wouldn't want to), 5 min Z2 recovery between
- 5 min cooldown
