# Training Plan: October 13 – October 26, 2026

**Focus:** First real block after the Oct 5–11 vacation week. Two shoulder-clean blocks
in a row (pull-ups to 3×8, full push-up superset, no pain flagged) means it's a
reasonable point to test overhead pressing again — cautiously, light, dumbbells
instead of barbell. Legs hold at the same proven numbers rather than progressing,
since recovery has been depressed most of the past week.

## Notes

- **Monday Oct 12 isn't part of this plan** — still easing back in post-vacation,
  picking structure back up Tuesday.
- **Overhead press reintroduction:** DB overhead press, light, placed right after KB
  row (fresh, not pre-fatigued) — same logic as the original post-SIRVA pull-up test:
  get a clean first read before anything else loads the shoulder. **Any pinch or sharp
  pain in the left shoulder → stop immediately, drop OH press for the rest of the
  block.** Planned at 15 lbs/hand for the re-test; actual Week 1 session went straight
  to 2×8 @ 40 lbs/hand (RPE 7.0) — well past the planned test weight, but confirmed
  clean, no shoulder pain. Treating 40 lbs as the real baseline now rather than
  repeating the original 15 lb plan.
- **Week 2 Session A holds rather than progresses further** — Week 1 also drifted to
  4×8 pull-ups and 4×15/12 push-ups (vs. the planned 3 sets), all clean too, but
  recovery cratered by the end of the week (37% on 10-19, after 48% and 43% the two
  days before — birthday week plus a 271-min MTB day on 10-18). Pulling Week 2 back to
  the originally planned 3-set volume for pull-ups/push-ups and holding OH press flat
  at 2×8 @ 40 lbs rather than adding more on top of an already-depressed recovery
  state. Further OH press load increases can wait for a block where recovery isn't
  compromised.
- **Squat/RDL/KB Swing hold at 115/135/155, 65/95/105, 35 lbs** — RPE has had headroom
  (~8, not 9–10) across multiple clean sessions, so there's room to progress, but
  recovery's been rough the last week (post-vacation dip, see below) — holding rather
  than progressing two things at once this block.
- **Recovery opening this block depressed.** Oct 5–11: 48% avg recovery, HRV 52.0 avg
  [41.9–65.3]. Four of seven days red (<50%): 10-06 (30%), 10-07 (41%), 10-09 (43%),
  10-11 (47%). Likely still paying for vacation efforts (263-min hike 9/30, 217-min MTB
  10/10) plus travel disruption. Standing policy: keep sessions on the calendar
  regardless of score — but don't read Week 1 numbers as a true baseline if recovery

- **Runs continue the ramp** — 45 min this week, 50 min next, toward the standing
  60-min goal. (Informal runs already hit 49 min on 10/11 — the plan ramp is
  intentionally a notch behind what's actually been run.)
- **Group rides and MTB always trump the plan.**
- Sat and Sun can swap — both are cardio slots.

## Strength Sessions

### Session A — Upper, overhead press re-test (~55 min)

**Warm-up (5 min) — keep it pain-free, not a test**
- Arm circles, band pull-aparts (light), 10 scapular push-ups, hip flexor stretch.
  Stop any warm-up movement that provokes the left shoulder.

**KB row (single arm) — first movement, lowest shoulder risk**
- 3 × 10 @ 40 lbs — unchanged, holding.

**DB Overhead Press — first test since removal**
- Week 1: 2 × 8 @ 15 lbs/hand. Done fresh, right after KB row, before fatigue — same
  reasoning as the original post-injury pull-up test. Any pinch/sharp pain in the left
  shoulder → stop here, drop OH press for the rest of the block.
- Week 2: 2 × 10 @ 15 lbs/hand **only if Week 1 was clean.** If Week 1 flagged
  anything, hold at 2×8 or drop entirely — don't push through pain.

**Pull-ups — holding at full volume**
- 3 × 8 @ bodyweight — same as last block's Week 2, not progressing further while OH
  press is the new variable being tested.

**Push-ups — full superset, holding**
- 3 × 15 standard + 3 × 12 incline — same as last block's Week 2.

**Standing Calf Raise — bodyweight only, no bar**
- 3 × 15 @ bodyweight, 2-sec up, pause at top, 2-sec down.

**Core finisher (both weeks)**
- 3 rounds: 9 hanging knee raises, 18 sec hollow hold
- 1 round push-up to downward dog
- 2 rounds: 70 sec plank (posterior tilt — tuck pelvis)

## Trainer tempo — rotating options

Default leans toward the structured options (Sweet Spot / Over-Unders) rather than
easy Z3 — pick whichever fits the day, but favor 1 or 2 unless recovery says otherwise.
If riding outdoors instead (or FTS MTB is on), no structure required.

**Option 1 — Sweet Spot**
- 10 min easy warm-up (Z2)
- 2 × 15 min sweet spot (top of Z3 / bottom of Z4), 5 min Z2 recovery between
- 10 min cooldown

**Option 2 — Over-Unders**
- 10 min easy warm-up (Z2)
- 3 × 10 min alternating 2 min over-threshold (Z4) / 2 min under (Z3), 5 min Z2
  recovery between sets
- 10 min cooldown

**Option 3 — Classic Z3 tempo (fallback / lower-key day)**
- 10 min easy warm-up (Z2)
- 4 × 8 min Z3 ("comfortably hard" — can talk, wouldn't want to), 5 min Z2 recovery
  between
- 5 min cooldown
